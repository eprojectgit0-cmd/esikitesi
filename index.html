We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers. For long-term projects, we also offer milestone-based payment plans to ensure flexibility for our clients.

Yes, you can upgrade or modify your project scope at any time. Just reach out to your dedicated project manager, and we will adjust the timeline and deliverables accordingly.
